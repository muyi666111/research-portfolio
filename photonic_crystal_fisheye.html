Maxwell fish-eye lens

I reconstructed a Maxwell fish-eye lens in 2D FDTD. A radial index profile brings light from an input port to the opposite side. In the discrete design, the rod radii or spacing approximate that profile.

Reference design by Gilarlue et al.
