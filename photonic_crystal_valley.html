Valley photonic crystal waveguide

I modeled two honeycomb air-hole lattices with swapped hole diameters on a 220 nm silicon layer. Joining the two domains along their large holes forms an interface that guides light.

At 1430 nm, the 2D model gives relative through powers of 71.5%, 71.2%, and 63.4% for the straight, Z-shaped, and Ω-shaped paths. A finite-time 3D check for the straight slab gives about 32%.

Reference design by He et al.
