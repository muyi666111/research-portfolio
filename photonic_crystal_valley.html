Photonic crystals

Dispersion & FDTD

Valley photonic crystal waveguide

I modeled two honeycomb air-hole lattices with swapped hole diameters on a 220 nm silicon layer. Joining the two domains along their large holes forms an interface that guides light.

Straight and bent paths

A separate, finite-time 3D calculation with the physical silicon-on-insulator slab gave about 32% relative through power for the straight interface at 1430 nm. The 3D result shows how strongly the transmission estimate depends on the model.

Reference design by He et al.
